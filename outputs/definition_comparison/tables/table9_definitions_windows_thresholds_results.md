**Thresholds are not single numbers.** Each is county- and calendar-specific and re-estimated every year, so the columns give what the percentile works out to in degF: the median over all counties x calendar keys x analysis years, and the Jun-Sep and Dec-Feb medians. The gap between those two is why a year-round relative definition flags cool-season days.

Counts suffixed (QA) are pooled across counties and are QA quantities, never substantive results; `days/county med` is the substantive county-level figure. Durations are integer days. All counts are cumulative over 2015-2025, never annual.
